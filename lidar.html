Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Drone LiDAR

Clear answers regarding survey deliverables, CAD/GIS integration, dense tree canopy penetration, and project pricing.

How does drone LiDAR penetrate dense East Texas pine and hardwood trees? +
Unlike traditional photogrammetry which only reconstructs the visible tree canopy surface, our flagship DJI Zenmuse L3 payload emits multi-return laser pulses capable of recording up to 16 discrete returns per pulse. As pulses pass through the foliage, returns slip through tiny gaps between needles and branches, while the final return bounces off the true ground surface. Our post-processing software filters out non-ground vegetation returns to expose an ultra-precise, bare-earth digital terrain model (DTM).
What exact CAD and GIS file formats will our engineering team receive? +
We deliver standard, fully classified geospatial deliverables ready for direct import into AutoCAD Civil 3D, Carlson Survey, Bentley MicroStation, QGIS, and ArcGIS. Standard packages include:
  • ASPRS Classified Point Cloud (.LAS / .LAZ with ground, vegetation, and structure classifications)
  • Bare-Earth Digital Terrain Model (32-bit floating point GeoTIFF DTM)
  • Topographic Elevation Contours (1-foot or 2-foot indexed polylines in .DXF, .DWG, and ESRI Shapefile .SHP)
  • Hydrology Flow & Drainage Shapefiles (Flow accumulation & basin boundaries)
  • RTK Checkpoint Calibration Report (Comprehensive PDF showing horizontal and vertical RMS error)
Does an aerial LiDAR survey replace a licensed land survey (PLS)? +
No. Our service provides high-accuracy remote sensing, topographic modeling, volumetric measurements, and civil design terrain data calibrated against on-site RTK checkpoints. It does not establish legal property boundary corners or constitute a legal boundary survey under Texas Board of Professional Land Surveying rules, and does not include a Professional Land Surveyor (PLS) seal unless arranged through a partner survey firm.
What is the typical turnaround time for deliverables? +
For standard parcels up to 500 contiguous acres, our turnaround time from flight completion to final CAD/GIS delivery is typically 3 to 5 business days. Expedited 48-hour turnarounds are available upon request for time-sensitive development or earthwork grading projects.
How does the $15/Acre promotional pricing package work? +
Our promotional package includes a minimum mobilization fee of $2,250, which covers up to 150 contiguous acres ($15/acre equivalent). Any contiguous acreage beyond 150 acres is billed at the same flat $15 per acre. Projects located within our primary 100-mile flight radius centered on Spring, TX incur zero travel or mileage surcharges.
What are the site access and airspace requirements? +
We require 1 to 2 unobstructed takeoff/landing locations that maintain legal Visual Line of Sight (VLOS) over the target parcel. We handle all FAA flight authorizations via automated LAANC or FAA airspace waivers. Weather conditions must be safe for flight: sustained winds below 20 mph, cloud ceilings above flight altitude, and zero precipitation. If bad weather delays operations, flights are rescheduled promptly at no additional fee.
