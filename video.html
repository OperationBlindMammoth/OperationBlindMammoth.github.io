Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment do you use for aerial videography? +
We fly a DJI Mavic 3 with a Hasselblad 4/3 CMOS sensor for cinematic 4K/5.1K aerial footage, and a DJI Avata 2 for smooth, immersive FPV indoor walkthrough tours and dynamic outdoor shots. Both platforms are insured for commercial operations.
Can you fly indoors for facility tours? +
Yes. The DJI Avata 2 is a compact, propeller-guarded FPV drone specifically designed for tight indoor spaces. It produces smooth, cinematic walkthrough videos of schools, warehouses, churches, event venues, and commercial facilities. We coordinate directly with facility managers to ensure safe, efficient indoor flights.
What's included in the shoot price? +
Shoot pricing covers all on-site flight time and all raw footage delivered digitally via secure cloud download. You receive full commercial rights to use the footage however you'd like. For projects that only need basic assembly, audio sync, and color correction, editing is a flat $100. Custom editing with storytelling, transitions, and motion graphics is quoted per project.
How quickly can I receive my footage? +
Raw footage is typically delivered within 24 hours of the shoot. Edited videos depend on project complexity but are usually delivered within 3–7 business days.
Do you need FAA clearance to fly? +
Curtis holds an FAA Part 107 Commercial Remote Pilot Certificate. All outdoor flights comply with FAA regulations, and airspace authorization (LAANC) is obtained automatically for any controlled airspace near airports. Indoor flights do not require FAA authorization.
What happens if weather cancels a shoot? +
Safety is non-negotiable. If sustained winds exceed 20 mph or precipitation is forecast during the shoot window, we reschedule at zero additional cost. We monitor weather closely and will proactively communicate any changes well before the scheduled time.
